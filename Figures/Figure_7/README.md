# Figure 7 - Protein Expression Sensitivity Analysis

## Overview

Figure 7 examines how **protein expression levels** affect metabolic behavior, specifically acetate excretion patterns across growth rates. The analysis explores:
- **Panel A**: Cytosolic protein expression (φ_Y) - varying cytoplasmic protein allocation
- **Panel B**: Membrane protein expression (φ_Z) - varying membrane protein allocation

Both panels show acetate flux vs growth rate at **γ = 0.25** (moderate membrane constraint).

**Key findings:**
- Higher protein expression levels shift acetate threshold
- Cytosolic vs membrane protein allocation have distinct effects on overflow metabolism
- Protein expression constraints interact with membrane surface area constraints
- Acetate excretion onset varies with protein allocation strategies

## Script

### `Figure_7_protein_expression.m` - Protein Expression Plots

**Purpose**: Generate acetate excretion plots showing the effect of protein expression levels.

**What it does:**
- Loads protein expression sensitivity data from `../../data/outputs/Protein_Expression/`
- Filters for MAFBA model, γ = 0.25
- **Panel A**: Plots acetate vs growth rate for 6 cytosolic protein levels (Y)
  - Y levels: 0%, 1%, 2%, 4%, 6%, 8%
  - Blue/green color palette
- **Panel B**: Plots acetate vs growth rate for 6 membrane protein levels (Z)
  - Z levels: 0%, 2%, 4%, 10%, 15%, 20%

- Detects and marks acetate threshold (λ_ac) for each condition
- Downsamples markers for clarity

**Outputs** (saved to `output/`):
- `iML1515_MAFBA_protExp_panel_A.svg/.png` (Cytosolic, φ_Y)
- `iML1515_MAFBA_protExp_panel_B.svg/.png` (Membrane, φ_Z)

## Understanding the Analysis

### Protein Expression Levels

**φ_Y (Cytosolic proteins):**
- Represents allocation of total protein budget to cytoplasmic enzymes
- Higher φ_Y → more metabolic capacity in cytosol
- Range: 0% to 8%

**φ_Z (Membrane proteins):**
- Represents allocation of total protein budget to membrane-bound proteins
- Higher φ_Z → more respiratory/transport capacity
- Range: 0% to 20%

**Trade-off:**
- Increasing Y or Z reduces protein available for other processes
- Affects growth rate and metabolic regime transitions
- Interacts with membrane surface area constraint (γ)

### Acetate Threshold (λ_ac)

The **acetate threshold** marks the growth rate where overflow metabolism begins:
- **Detection method**: Identifies when membrane flux (ATP synthase) saturates
- **Biological significance**: Transition from pure respiration to mixed respiration + fermentation
- **Visual**: Vertical dashed lines in each plot

**Interpretation:**
- Lower λ_ac → acetate excretion starts at slower growth
- Higher λ_ac → cell can maintain pure respiration to faster growth

### Parameters

## Technical Notes

### Acetate Threshold Detection Algorithm

The script uses a slope-based method to detect λ_ac:

1. Calculate slope of membrane flux (ATP synthase) vs growth rate
2. Find maximum slope (peak respiratory capacity)
3. Identify where slope drops below threshold (membrane saturation)
4. Locate first point with acetate > 0.01 mmol gDW⁻¹ h⁻¹
5. Fallback strategies if threshold not clearly detected

This method identifies the **metabolic regime transition** from pure respiration to overflow metabolism.

### Protein Expression Levels

**Y (Cytosolic):**
- Lower levels (0-2%): Minimal impact
- Higher levels (4-8%): Significant metabolic shifts
- Color gradient: Green → Blue (increasing)

**Z (Membrane):**
- Lower levels (0-4%): Moderate impact
- Higher levels (10-20%): Strong metabolic shifts
